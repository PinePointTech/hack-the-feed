THESIS: One page that reads like a contact sheet going on air. The public sees what is trending, checks any claim, then watches the analysis take over in a signal band. Creators get a route to make their own debunk. The category default (a headline, a card row, a button) is refused.

OWN-WORLD: A light paper ground, ink, and one marker-lime accent used only as a highlighter behind one keyword and on the Trending sticker. Bricolage Grotesque for headlines and UI, Source Serif 4 bold italic for one keyword per headline and for analysis text, Azeret Mono for numbers. A Swiss grid with playful breaks: overlapping frames, a dashed course route, and a graticule behind the analysis.

STORY: A visitor sees the trending myths as frames, types a claim or taps a myth, reads the verdict and its sources in the analysis band, then follows the creator route to Creator mode.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 1440. A pill app bar with MyBuster, Calendar, and Creator mode. Left column: the headline "Find the health myth. Get the receipts. Make the debunk." with "receipts" in bold italic serif on a lime highlight, the primary button "See this week's myths", and a search field "Check a health claim" under it. Right column: three staggered contact-sheet frames for this week's trending myths. The primary button and the search field sit above the first fold.

FORM: One page in a fixed order: trending frames, featured debunk with the AI presenter, signal band, creator route, footer. Pinned by the user (the proof sheet on top, the signal bench below), so no roll was run. seed: f2fae95906ec3821
