# Compound.mov

Owner: Vi. A Mac app that edits and posts short videos using your own Claude Code, Codex or Cursor plan. Site: https://compound.mov (download at https://compound.mov/download, help at https://compound.mov/support).

## Facts (from the site)
- Mac with Apple Silicon, macOS 15 or later. iPhone app too.
- Compound Pro: $20 per month, 14-day trial, card needed. 1,000 credits a month for captions and posting, 6 social accounts, 100 GB of storage.
- Does: edits (cuts, zooms, extra shots), captions, turns podcasts into shorts, makes no-camera videos with moving graphics, writes scripts and hooks, posts to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X and LinkedIn, shows stats, and sets up comment-to-DM links.
- No public API found. The organizers said they will hand out an API key; ask what it can do. Contact from our notes: isaac@compound.mov.
- Prize: the Compound Viral Award, an extra $1,000 for the video with the most views within 24 hours.

## Our flow
1. Before 3:45: install, start the trial, connect the TikTok or Instagram account you will post from.
2. About 4:05: record the approved script (iPhone or webcam, 9:16, 30 to 40 s). Read it from the studio's "Copy script".
3. About 4:15: in Compound, cut the pauses, add captions, zoom on the key lines, put each line's `on_screen` text on screen, and end with "Receipts: link in bio".
4. Export a 9:16 MP4 to `apps/web/public/video/lymphatic-detox.mp4` and attach it in the studio so Kris's QA runs on it (by 4:30).
5. If QA passes and you want the Viral Award: post through Compound with the receipts link (`?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=bio`). A comment-to-DM link ("comment RECEIPTS") is a nice touch.

## Rules
- Never put other creators' footage in our video. Use a quote card (handle, exact quote, timestamp, link) or the platform's native stitch or duet.
- Every on-screen claim must match an approved line; QA checks this.
