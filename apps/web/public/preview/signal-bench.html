01 False Fixture data

Lymphatic drainage detoxes you and melts fat

Verdict

There's no good evidence that lymphatic drainage detoxes you or causes weight loss. It can reduce swelling for a while, and it plays a real role in treating lymphedema.

Open the receipts

What is true

  • It can reduce swelling and give a temporary slimmer, tighter look (UCLA Health).
  • It plays a critical role in treating lymphedema (UCLA Health).

What is false

  • That it causes weight loss. There is no evidence for that (UCLA Health, National Geographic).
  • That it gets rid of toxins. Those claims are unfounded (National Geographic).

Sources

  • UCLA Health Lymphatic drainage massage - separating fact from fiction
  • National Geographic What lymphatic drainage massage actually does for your body
